HOW TO PLAY

Each round you're randomly assigned LONG or SHORT. Climb the ladder to set a STOP and a LIMIT — both at least 3 ticks from entry. Once locked, the tape starts scrolling: run across candles in your favor, jump the ones stacked against you. Clear an obstacle for profit, miss it and it costs you. Bank your LIMIT and the run continues, a little harder. Hit your STOP and the run is over.

←→ run
↑ jump
WS move ladder cursor (while setting stop/limit)
SPACE confirm stop, then confirm limit
R start a new run (resets score)

On a touch device, the same actions are mirrored by the on-screen buttons at the bottom of the screen.

The stop defines the loss before the round even starts. The tape doesn't know, and doesn't care, which side you took.
