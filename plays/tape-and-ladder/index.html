Read the marks, not just the colors.
Green candles close higher; red candles close lower. Amber brackets mark a jump obstacle. Wicks are decoration. Small candles can still change P&L when you pass them, even in the air.

Watch what comes next. Hold through a volume cluster or a news gap. Stay low under resistance; jump hostile support. Later, fans and moving-average channels add new timing challenges. The cue below the chart calls them out.

Your score is your highest P&L. Every gain and loss is multiplied by leverage. A loss from your peak uses up margin; exceed the allowance and the run ends. This is an arcade scoring rule.
